Tiny Swords: Realm War

Top-down RTS. No hero unit: command workers, buildings, armies, towers and flags.

Camera
WASD / arrows pan, mouse edge pan, wheel zoom.
Select
Left click one thing, drag-select army, Shift adds.
Orders
Right click ground to move, resources to harvest, enemies to attack, towers to garrison archers.
Build
Press B, choose a building, left click to place, Esc cancels.
Training
Select Castle/Barracks/Archery/Monastery, then use action buttons or hotkeys 1-5.
Rally / Flag
Select a production building and right click ground to set its flag. New units walk there.
Towers
Towers need an archer inside. Select archer(s), right click a tower, or use Garrison.
Minimap
Click to jump camera. Press M to expand/collapse.
Other
H help, Space pause, Esc cancel/close, Ctrl+A selects army, 0 selects all units.
